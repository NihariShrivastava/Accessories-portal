PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
The Accessories Portal is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade web application designed
for automobile dealerships and service centers to streamline end-to-end accessory
sales, inventory control, cash flow reconciliation, multi-tier approvals, fraud 
prevention, and financial auditing.

The system bridges communication and operational workflows across 7 distinct user 
roles (Administrators, Sales Counters, Team Leads, Cashiers, Billing Desks, Auditors, 
and Warehouses). It enforces strict accountability, eliminates billing discrepancies, 
tracks inventory depletion in real time, and automates dealership ledger reporting.

2. USER ROLES & ACCESS CONTROL MATRIX
================================================================================

The portal implements a strict role-based access control (RBAC) structure:

1. ADMINISTRATOR ('admin')
   - Complete operational and financial authority.
   - User Management: Create, update, assign, and delete Counters, Warehouses, 
     Team Leads, Cashiers, Auditors, and Billing Counters.
   - Inventory Management: Bulk Excel upload with custom timestamps, stock adjustments, 
     inter-counter/warehouse cart transfers, and batch deletion by upload date.
   - Comprehensive Analytics: Access to all 10+ ledger and reporting slides with Excel export.
   - Financial Overrides: Admin drawer adjustments, bill approvals/reversals, 
     and duplicate audit resolutions.

2. SALES COUNTER ('counter')
   - Frontline point-of-sale operator at dealership showroom or service counter.
   - Accessory Sales: Model-wise accessory catalog search, multi-item cart management, 
     stock verification, and tax calculations (CGST + SGST).
   - Invoicing: Captures customer name, phone, customer ID, vehicle chassis number, 
     engine number, checklist number; supports split payment methods (Cash, UPI, Card, 
     Bank Transfer, Finance).
   - Invoices: Instant branded invoice generation and thermal/A4 PDF downloading.
   - Physical Cash Drawer: Real-time balance calculation and submitting cash handover 
     requests to assigned cashiers.
   - Stock Visibility: Quick filter for shortage (stock <= 5) and surplus items.

3. TEAM LEAD ('team_lead')
   - Field supervisor assigned to specific showroom counters and warehouses.
   - Approval Queue: Reviews pending bills submitted by assigned counters.
   - Discounts & Excess: Authorizes customer discounts or excess adjustments with notes.
   - Stock Transfers: Coordinates stock replenishment from warehouses to sales counters.
   - Performance Monitoring: Views assigned counters' revenue, inventory shortages, 
     cashier collections, and accessory movements.

4. BILLING COUNTER ('billing_counter')
   - Administrative billing workstation responsible for invoice closure.
   - Workstation Queue: Receives approved bills from Team Leads/Admin.
   - System Reconciliation: Records Customer ID, verifies official dealership DMS / 
     Excellon Receipt Numbers, and validates payment UTRs.
   - Fraud / Duplicacy Detection: Real-time global collision scanner detecting duplicated 
     Excellon receipt numbers or bank UTR numbers across historical bills.
   - Finalization: Marks bills as 'closed' to pass into the auditor queue.

5. CASHIER ('cashier')
   - Central financial desk managing store cash flows across assigned counters.
   - Handover Acceptance: Reviews and approves/reverts pending cash handovers from counters.
   - Operational Expenses: Logs daily store cash outflows (Electricity, Refreshments/Chai, 
     Office Supplies, Fuel/Travel, Maintenance, Miscellaneous).
   - Bank Deposits: Records cash deposited to bank accounts with Bank Name, Account 
     Number, and IFSC code.
   - Refunds: Records customer cash refunds linked to target counters.
   - Real-Time Drawer: Maintains accurate cash-in-drawer balance.

6. AUDITOR ('auditor')
   - Internal compliance and financial auditor overseeing assigned Team Leads & Counters.
   - Auditing Workstation: Reviews closed bills for discrepancies.
   - Dealership Savings Engine: Verifies base price discounts, miscellaneous savings, 
     TL excess, and calculates net Dealership Savings and payment gaps.
   - Revert / Forward: Reverts erroneous bills back to Team Lead with remarks or 
     forwards verified audits to Admin.
   - Edit Metadata: Ability to correct customer and payment reference metadata.

7. WAREHOUSE MANAGER ('warehouse')
   - Storage depot personnel monitoring physical stock.
   - Inventory Catalog: Real-time view of all accessories, part numbers, vehicle models, 
     retail prices, and color-coded stock availability indicators (Green >5, Amber 1-5, Red 0).

3. END-TO-END BUSINESS WORKFLOW & LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

[Step 1: Inventory Stocking & Upload]
   - Admin uploads an Excel file (.xlsx) containing columns: Model, Accessory Name, 
     Part Number, Price, CGST %, SGST %, Quantity, and Target Counter/Warehouse.
   - System performs a batch upsert into the 'accessories' table.
   - Stock quantities become immediately visible to Counters, Team Leads, and Warehouses.

[Step 2: Cart Building & Bill Generation]
   - Counter operator selects a vehicle model (e.g. Elevate, City, Amaze).
   - Operator adds items and required quantities to the cart. Stock availability is verified.
   - Operator inputs customer details (Name, Phone, ID) and Vehicle Details (Chassis, 
     Engine, Checklist #).
   - Payment is configured: Single mode or Split Payment (e.g., ₹5,000 Cash + ₹15,000 UPI).
   - System generates a formatted sequential Bill Number (e.g., "INV-0042" or counter prefix 
     derived from operator initials + ID hash).
   - The invoice rows are inserted into 'bills' table; stock is decremented immediately.
   - Initial bill status: approval_status = 'pending'.

[Step 3: Team Lead Supervisory Approval]
   - Team Lead opens 'Bill Approvals' on their dashboard.
   - TL reviews billed accessories, prices, and customer info.
   - If requested, TL enters approved discount amount or excess adjustment with a remark.
   - TL clicks "Approve Bill" -> approval_status changes to 'approved'.

[Step 4: Billing Counter Finalization & Anti-Duplicacy Check]
   - The approved bill appears on Billing Counter's 'Workstation'.
   - Billing clerk verifies payment against DMS (Excellon / Dealer Management System) 
     and attaches the Excellon Receipt Number and Customer ID.
   - Anti-Duplicacy engine runs automatically. If an Excellon Receipt Number or UTR has 
     already been used on another bill, a warning banner flags the conflict.
   - Billing clerk closes the bill -> approval_status changes to 'closed'.

[Step 5: Cash Handover & Cashier Drawer Management]
   - Counter operator collects physical cash from customers into their local counter drawer.
   - At end of shift/day, counter submits a 'Cashier Transfer' transaction via 'Cash Drawer'.
   - Cashier sees the transfer under 'Pending Handovers' and clicks 'Approve'.
   - Physical cash moves to Cashier's Central Drawer.
   - Cashier logs daily expenses or bank deposit vouchers, keeping the ledger balanced.

[Step 6: Compliance Audit & Dealership Savings]
   - The 'closed' bill appears in the Auditor's Queue.
   - Auditor opens 'Audit Receipt', inspects payment breakdown, references, and UTRs.
   - Auditor computes: Total Savings = Base Price Savings + Misc Savings + TL Excess.
   - Computes Total Gap = Sum of payment gaps.
   - If satisfied, Auditor clicks "Forward to Admin" -> audit_status = 'audited' / 'verified'.
   - If discrepancy exists, Auditor clicks "Revert to Team Lead" with feedback comments.

[Step 7: Administrative Oversight & Multi-Slide Executive Reports]
   - Admin monitors the entire operation via 10 interactive reporting slides.
   - Reports include: Ledger, Revenue, Inventory Shortage/Surplus, Amount Collected 
     (by payment mode), Team Lead approvals, Billing Counter throughput, Cashier 
     balances, Auditor savings, Unpaid Bills, and Accessory Movement.
   - Admin can export any report to Excel with one click.

4. DATABASE SCHEMA & DATA DICTIONARY
================================================================================

1. TABLE: profiles
   - id: UUID (Primary Key, matches Supabase auth.users.id)
   - name: TEXT (Display name of the user/counter/branch)
   - username: TEXT (Unique login handle)
   - password: TEXT (Stored for administrative staff provisioning)
   - role: TEXT ('admin' | 'counter' | 'team_lead' | 'cashier' | 'auditor' | 'billing_counter' | 'warehouse')
   - assigned_counters: UUID[] (Array of counter profile IDs assigned to TL/Cashier/Billing Counter)
   - assigned_warehouses: UUID[] (Array of warehouse profile IDs assigned to TL)
   - assigned_team_leads: UUID[] (Array of team lead IDs assigned to Auditor)
   - created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ

2. TABLE: accessories
   - id: UUID (Primary Key)
   - counter_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> profiles.id; counter or warehouse location)
   - vehicle_model: TEXT (e.g., 'Elevate', 'City 5th Gen', 'Amaze')
   - name: TEXT (Accessory item name, e.g., 'Seat Cover Elegance')
   - accessory_code: TEXT (OEM part number / SKU)
   - quantity: INTEGER (Available stock count)
   - price: NUMERIC (Retail selling price before tax)
   - purchase_price: NUMERIC (Wholesale cost / dealer procurement cost)
   - cgst_percent: NUMERIC (Central GST rate, e.g., 9%)
   - sgst_percent: NUMERIC (State GST rate, e.g., 9%)
   - created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ

3. TABLE: bills
   - id: UUID (Primary Key)
   - bill_number: TEXT (Formatted invoice ID, e.g., 'INV-0021' or 'INV-0021-1')
   - counter_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> profiles.id)
   - accessory_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> accessories.id)
   - vehicle_model: TEXT
   - customer_name: TEXT
   - customer_phone: TEXT
   - customer_id: TEXT (DMS Customer Identifier)
   - chassis_number: TEXT (Vehicle VIN / Chassis Number)
   - engine_number: TEXT
   - checklist_number: TEXT
   - quantity: INTEGER (Items sold in this row)
   - base_amount: NUMERIC (Taxable value)
   - cgst_amount: NUMERIC (CGST component)
   - sgst_amount: NUMERIC (SGST component)
   - total_amount: NUMERIC (Gross invoice value)
   - payment_method: TEXT ('Cash' | 'UPI' | 'Card' | 'Bank Transfer' | 'Finance' | 'Split Payment')
   - payment_details: JSONB (Array of payment objects: method, amount, utr, excellon_receipt_number)
   - amount_paid: NUMERIC (Amount collected)
   - amount_left: NUMERIC (Outstanding balance / credit)
   - excellon_receipt_number: TEXT (Official dealer receipt number)
   - approval_status: TEXT ('pending' | 'approved' | 'closed' | 'reverted' | 'reverted_by_admin')
   - excess_adjustment: NUMERIC (Additional adjustment approved by TL)
   - discount_approved: NUMERIC (Discount conceded by TL)
   - approval_note: TEXT (Remark by supervisor)
   - audit_status: TEXT ('pending' | 'audited' | 'verified' | 'reverted')
   - auditor_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> profiles.id)
   - total_savings: NUMERIC (Dealership savings calculated during audit)
   - total_gap: NUMERIC (Payment deficiency recorded)
   - dealership_savings: JSONB (Detailed savings breakdown)
   - payment_audits: JSONB (Historical audit log and resolved duplicate patches)
   - created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ

4. TABLE: drawer_transactions
   - id: UUID (Primary Key)
   - counter_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> profiles.id; source counter or cashier ID)
   - transaction_type: TEXT ('cashier_transfer' | 'daily_expense' | 'bank_transfer' | 'refund')
   - amount: NUMERIC (Monetary transaction value)
   - status: TEXT ('pending' | 'approved' | 'reverted')
   - category: TEXT (Expense category: 'Electricity Bill', 'Chai / Refreshments', 'Office Supplies', etc.)
   - details: TEXT (Remarks, reason, or Admin Adjustment tag)
   - bank_name: TEXT (For bank transfers)
   - account_number: TEXT
   - ifsc_code: TEXT
   - created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ

5. TABLE: login_logs
   - id: UUID (Primary Key)
   - user_id: UUID (Foreign Key -> profiles.id)
   - created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ (Timestamp of login)

5. KEY ARCHITECTURAL HIGHLIGHTS & SMART ALGORITHMS
================================================================================

A. VIRTUAL DOMAIN MULTI-REALM AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Users log in with a clean, simple username (e.g. 'counter1', 'lead_north', 'admin').
Under the hood, the system iterates through internal virtual email domains:
  ['portal.com', 'teamlead.com', 'cashier.com', 'auditor.com', 
   'portal.local', 'teamlead.local', 'cashier.local', 'auditor.local']
It transparently binds the username to Supabase Auth, retrieves the user metadata 
and profile, verifies active account status, logs the login event to 'login_logs', 
and routes to the appropriate dashboard with zero user friction.

B. DYNAMIC SEQUENTIAL INVOICE GENERATOR WITH COLLISION RETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bill numbers are formed deterministically using counter operator initials plus a 
3-character user hash (e.g., "NS3A9-0001") or standard "INV-" prefixes.
To guarantee collision-free concurrency in high-volume showrooms:
- It queries the highest existing sequence number for that counter prefix.
- Loops with retry attempts incrementing `maxNum + 1 + retryCount`.
- Multi-item cart bills share the base bill number with index suffixes (-1, -2).
- Financial totals (amount_paid, amount_left) are pinned to row 0 to prevent 
  double-counting in SQL aggregate sums.

C. REAL-TIME FRAUD & DUPLICACY DETECTION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located in the Billing Counter module and Admin Reports:
- Maps all historical bills across all counters by `excellon_receipt_number` and `utr_number`.
- Identifies any instance where the same bank UTR or DMS receipt has been submitted 
  more than once across different customers or dates.
- Flags duplicate rows in high-visibility amber/red badges, preventing duplicate 
  claims or false revenue reporting.

D. COMPREHENSIVE CASH DRAWER FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cashier Drawer Balance is dynamically computed in real-time:
  Drawer Balance = (Approved Cashier Transfers from Counters)
                 + (Admin Cash Adjustments)
                 - (Approved Daily Expenses)
                 - (Approved Bank Deposits)
                 - (Approved Customer Refunds)

Counter Local Drawer Balance:
  Counter Drawer = (Total Cash Collected from Cash Bills)
                 - (Approved & Pending Transfers to Cashier)

E. MULTI-SLIDE EXECUTIVE REPORTING ENGINE (10 SLIDES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ledger: Counter-by-counter summary of total bills, receivable amount (debit), 
   total collected (credit), outstanding balance, and cash drawer balance.
2. Revenue Report: Total items sold and gross revenue generated per counter.
3. Inventory Report: Comprehensive count of surplus items and shortage items (<= 5 units).
4. Amount Collected: Strict breakdown across Cash, UPI, Card, and Bank Transfer.
5. Team Lead Report: Assigned counters, pending approvals, approved discounts & excess.
6. Billing Counter Report: Assigned counters, pending bills, closed bills count.
7. Cashier Report: Pending handovers, approved handovers, expenses, drawer balance.
8. Auditor Report: Pending audits, approved audits, total dealership savings discovered.
9. Unpaid Bills Report: Direct drill-down into bills with remaining balances.
10. Accessory Movement Report: Inflow vs outflow counts grouped by vehicle model.

7. ENVIRONMENT CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

The application requires the following environment variables in a `.env` file at the root:

VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://<your-project-ref>.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=<your-anon-public-key>

Notes:
- `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`: The HTTPS endpoint of your hosted Supabase instance.
- `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`: The public anonymous key for client requests (protected by RLS).

9. SECURITY & DATA INTEGRITY BEST PRACTICES IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

1. Session Isolation:
   Supabase client uses `window.sessionStorage` rather than `localStorage`. This guarantees
   that closing the tab/browser terminates the session and prevents cross-tab role bleed.

2. Concurrency Control:
   Stock deductions occur in real time upon bill submission. If multiple items are in a cart,
   pre-validation ensures all items have sufficient quantities before any row is committed.

3. Immutable Financial Ledgers:
   Counter operators cannot delete or retroactively modify bills. Discrepancies must be 
   escalated through Team Leads, Billing Desks, Auditors, or Administrators.

4. Duplicate Prevention:
   Payment details (Excellon DMS numbers and Bank UTR strings) are actively monitored 
   across all historical transactions to prevent fraudulent double-claiming.

5. Role Authorization Guards:
   Direct URL tampering is intercepted by client-side `<ProtectedRoute allowedRoles={[...]}>`
   wrappers that verify profile metadata and redirect unauthorized requests to their 
   home dashboard.
